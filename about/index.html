About

Hi, I am Tato. I enjoy technology and in the last years I’ve been digging deeply into Cybersecurity, mostly on the offensive side. I started in security really early in my life, learning reverse engineering from old PDFs and crackmes after school (such a nostalgic memory). Right after Informatics was the logical choice for me.

And here I am, after some years not much has changed, but I can see with different eyes on how important it is to share knowledge. That’s the main reason I started this blog. I want to make this space something I will enjoy reading in the future and make it public so anyone who can find something useful would take it, and embrace it.

Besides technology, my interests also include nerdy things like, watching Sci-Fi movies, comic based movies :D. Also I am really into doing sports, things like crossfit and weightlifting these years hit me a lot, I found in exercise a good balance between these long hours sitting at a desk.

So with that in mind, I will post mostly technology related content, and from time to time also some personal (Hobbies) I do or find interesting in my free time.
